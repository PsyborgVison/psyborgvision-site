## Section 02 — How this works

> Two-column or stacked block. Plain, short paragraphs. No "passionate" or "innovative" anywhere.

```
SECTION EYEBROW:        // how it works

PARAGRAPH 01 — No middle layer
When you hire PsyborgVision, you talk to me. The same person doing the build is the
one who answered your first email and will be the one shipping the final files.
There's no project manager translating, no junior dev quietly outsourcing, no agency
overhead baked into the quote.

PARAGRAPH 02 — One scope, one price
I quote per project, not per hour. You get a fixed number, a clear scope, and
a delivery window. Anything outside scope gets discussed before it gets billed.

PARAGRAPH 03 — Code-to-plastic
Most freelancers do one thing. I do two: web (sites, apps, ongoing maintenance)
and 3D printing (custom or catalog). Some clients want both. Most want one. Either
works — but if you've ever needed a website AND a physical thing for the same
launch and had to coordinate two separate vendors, you know why this combo exists.
```
