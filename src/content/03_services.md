## Section 03 — How it works

> Process strip. Mono-styled steps. Reads as a checklist.

```
SECTION EYEBROW:        // process

STEP 01 — Inquiry
  You send a description, links, references, or whatever you have.
  Plus your timeline and budget if you have one.

STEP 02 — Quote
  I come back with a real number, scope, and timeline. Usually within 48 hours.

STEP 03 — Kickoff
  Half down to start. We agree on milestones, then I get to work.

STEP 04 — Build
  Regular check-ins (Slack, email, or call — your preference). Drafts shared
  as they're ready.

STEP 05 — Delivery
  Final delivery. Files, deployment, walkthrough, or shipped product —
  depending on the job. Half due on completion.

STEP 06 — After
  Web projects: 30 days of bug fixes included. Prints: replacement on print
  defects within 14 days. Retainer clients: ongoing care.
```
